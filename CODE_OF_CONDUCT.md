# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, patient, and specific. Welcome people with different backgrounds and experience. Critique the work and its evidence, not the person. Give credit and respect licenses, privacy, and the right to decline a request.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of private information, spam, and malicious submissions are not welcome. Do not post another person's credentials or private data even when reporting a problem.

Maintainers may edit/remove disruptive content or restrict participation to protect the project and its contributors. Report concerns through an appropriate available private GitHub channel; avoid reposting harmful/private material in a public thread. Apply the same standards in issues, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.
